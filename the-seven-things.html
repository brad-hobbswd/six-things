Proverbs 6:16–19 · KJV

The Seven Things

Seven serious warnings that call every believer to examine the heart.

1

A Proud Look

Pride places the self above correction, humility, and the dignity of others.

2

A Lying Tongue

Dishonesty corrupts trust and distorts the truth in relationships and community.

3

Hands That Shed Innocent Blood

Violence against innocent life and grave injustice carry profound moral weight.

4

A Heart That Deviseth Wicked Imaginations

Deliberate inward planning toward harm reveals the direction of the heart.

5

Feet Swift in Running to Mischief

6

A False Witness That Speaketh Lies

False testimony against another person can produce injustice and lasting consequences.

7

He That Soweth Discord Among Brethren

Deliberately cultivating division damages fellowship and the responsibility believers share.

These descriptions are brief introductions, not substitutes for the full biblical and theological discussion. Godly correction, truthful reporting, and responsible accountability must not be confused with manipulation or deliberate division.
