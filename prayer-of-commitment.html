The Seven Things

Prayer of Commitment

Lord, Examine My Heart

Heavenly Father,

I come before You with a heart that desires to reflect Your character and walk according to Your Word. I recognize that Your instruction is not given merely for me to identify the shortcomings of others, but to guide my own thoughts, decisions, and conduct. Search my heart and reveal anything within me that does not reflect Your righteousness.

Teach me to recognize pride before it influences my treatment of others. Guard my tongue against dishonesty, manipulation, and words that damage another person’s reputation. Keep my hands from injustice, my heart from malicious intentions, and my feet from pursuing anything that contradicts Your will. Give me the courage to speak truthfully, the wisdom to exercise discernment, and the humility to accept correction when I have been wrong.

Forgive me for the times I have contributed to division, entertained harmful thoughts, or allowed personal feelings to influence the way I treated someone. Help me take responsibility for my actions and demonstrate genuine repentance through changed conduct.

Give our churches leaders who serve with humility, generations who respect one another, and congregations that value truth, compassion, and accountability. Teach us to preserve what is good, correct what is harmful, and remain faithful to the principles of Your Word.

May we never become so concerned with correcting others that we neglect the condition of our own hearts. Let our words reflect truth, our actions demonstrate love, and our lives bring honor to the name of Jesus Christ.

Above everything, let Your Word remain the authority by which we examine ourselves. May we become people who practice what we teach, demonstrate what we believe, and represent You faithfully in our homes, churches, and communities.

In the mighty name of Jesus Christ,

Amen.
