About the Author

Bradley Hobbs is a writer, educator, and Christian voice committed to exploring the relationship between biblical truth, spiritual responsibility, and everyday life.

Through his writing, he addresses subjects that challenge readers to look beyond appearances, examine familiar beliefs, and consider whether their actions reflect the teachings of Jesus Christ.

His work places particular emphasis on faith, accountability, compassion, and the responsibility believers carry when representing the name of Christ. He believes Scripture should never become a tool for personal superiority, intimidation, or division. Rather, God’s Word should lead people toward truth, humility, repentance, and transformation.

In The Seven Things: When Scripture Becomes a Weapon and Religion Forgets Its Own Reflection, Bradley invites readers to reconsider Proverbs 6:16–19 as more than a passage used to identify wrongdoing in others. The seven behaviors described in these verses demand personal examination, regardless of generation, church position, reputation, or religious tradition.

His hope is that readers will approach Scripture with renewed reverence, recognize the importance of accountability, and carry forward a faith that reflects the character of Jesus Christ.

The Word deserves more than our agreement. Our lives should reflect the truth we profess.
