# The Seven Things

**By Bradley Hobbs**

A biblical examination of Proverbs 6:16–19, exploring the character, conduct, relationships, and spiritual responsibilities addressed in Solomon’s words.

## About the Book

Proverbs 6:16–19 identifies seven behaviors that the Lord declares detestable: a proud look, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood, a heart that devises wicked imaginations, feet that are swift in running to mischief, a false witness that speaks lies, and one who sows discord among brethren.

This book considers the passage within the wisdom literature of Scripture and follows its implications into personal conduct, church life, spiritual leadership, generational traditions, and the historical experience of religious communities. Its purpose is not to turn the passage into a list of accusations against others, but to encourage honest examination under the authority of the Word of God.

The discussion seeks to distinguish biblical instruction from human custom and personal interpretation. Scripture is approached in context, with supporting passages placed alongside the teaching they illuminate. The central responsibility is to receive the Word, examine ourselves by its truth, and live accordingly.

## Biblical Foundation

The primary passage is Proverbs 6:16–19. Supporting Scripture is included throughout the chapters in connection with the teaching being discussed. Biblical quotations are presented in continuous reading format, while Scripture references identify the passages for further study.

The book encourages readers to examine biblical teaching in its literary, historical, and spiritual context. Human traditions, institutional practices, titles, and personal interpretations remain accountable to Scripture.

## Historical References

Chapter Nine includes historical discussion and a list of sources for further reading. Those references are provided on the chapter page and should be consulted when exploring the historical material in greater depth.
