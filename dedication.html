The Seven Things

Dedication

To every believer who has ever been wounded by words spoken in the name of God, yet chose not to abandon the One whose name was spoken.

To those who have questioned traditions, challenged religious expectations, and searched Scripture for truth while navigating the weight of generations before them.

To the elders who faithfully served, the leaders who chose humility, and the generations willing to correct what needs correcting while preserving what remains good.

May we never become so committed to defending our religious traditions that we forget the heart of the God we claim to serve.

Above all, may this book honor Jesus Christ, whose truth exposes the heart, whose mercy calls us to repentance, and whose example teaches us how to love.

May the Word examine every generation, beginning with our own.
