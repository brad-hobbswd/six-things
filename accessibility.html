Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 2026

Our commitment

Bradley Hobbs aims to make this website readable and usable by as many visitors as possible, including people who use assistive technologies.

Accessibility practices

The website is being developed with semantic HTML, descriptive page titles, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus styles, responsive layouts, text alternatives for meaningful images, and consideration for reduced-motion preferences.

Ongoing improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Some content or features may not yet meet every visitor's needs, and improvements will be made as issues are identified.

Feedback

A verified contact method will be added when available. Visitors may then use that channel to report accessibility barriers and request information in an alternative format.

This statement describes the intended design practices and is not a certification of conformance with any accessibility standard.
