The Seven Things

Acknowledgments

Above all, I give honor and gratitude to Jesus Christ, whose Word remains the foundation of my faith and the standard by which every heart must be examined. The message of this book begins and ends with the responsibility to live what we profess, to practice what we preach, and to extend the same grace and accountability we expect from others.

I am grateful for the generations who carried the faith forward, served their churches, raised families in the knowledge of God, and preserved traditions that strengthened their communities. Their sacrifices deserve recognition. Their examples also remind us that every generation has a responsibility to examine what has been received and determine what should be carried forward.

My appreciation extends to those who have experienced the difficult side of religious culture, especially those who have struggled to reconcile the love of Christ with the actions of people who represented His name. May these pages encourage honest reflection, renewed faith, and the courage to pursue healing without abandoning the truth of Scripture.

To every reader willing to look beyond familiar traditions, respected titles, and established reputations, thank you for allowing this message to challenge your understanding. The purpose of these pages is not to condemn a generation or discredit the Church. The purpose is to call every believer, regardless of age or position, back to the standard of God’s Word.

May we become people who handle Scripture with reverence, exercise authority with humility, speak with truth, pursue peace with integrity, and demonstrate the character of Jesus Christ.

May the Word examine us before we use the Word to examine someone else.
